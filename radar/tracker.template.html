How ratings work

Act now

Binds NBFCs and changes something you run: lending, collections, KYC, fair practices. Also any circular whose title names digital lending, recovery agents, the Fair Practices Code, responsible business conduct, co-lending or KYC and binds NBFCs.

Worth a look

Binds NBFCs, but the change is routine, like a sanctions-list update for the KYC team. Or a big change on a payment rail NBFCs use.

Low

Touches NBFCs indirectly, such as payment operators or banks on a shared rail.

Not for NBFCs

Addressed to banks, cooperative banks, payment operators or forex dealers only.

Score out of 100 = who it applies to (1.0 NBFCs, 0.6 banks on a shared rail, 0.3 payment operators, 0.1 banks or forex) × topic weight (up to 60) × type of change (1.6 new rules, 1.3 amendment, 1.0 clarification, 0.7 list update). Act now at 60 or above, worth a look 30–59, low 10–29.
